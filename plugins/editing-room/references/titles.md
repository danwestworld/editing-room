# YouTube titles and thumbnails that work (reference for Packaging Pick)

Read this before writing any title. It holds the patterns behind real YouTube outliers from
coaches, consultants and creator-business channels, with live examples.

**Ratio** = how far a video beat that creator's own normal (their median recent video). 9.4x means
9.4 times their usual. A small channel's breakout ranks next to a big channel's. Data: 13 coaching,
business, fitness and creator channels plus 3 sampled competitors, measured November 2026.

## How to use this

1. Find the video's ONE promise first (what the viewer gets or avoids). Patterns wrap a promise;
   they never replace one.
2. Pick 3-4 patterns below that fit the video, and write titles in the creator's own words.
3. Stack two devices at most. A device = a number, a parenthetical, a timeframe, a named person,
   an age, a call-out. Three or more reads as keyword stuffing. Count the parenthetical in the
   60 characters.
4. Honesty rule: only use numbers, results and names the creator actually has. No result yet →
   use a pattern with no number (call-out, hard-until, command, contrarian, hypothetical reset).
   A title the video doesn't pay off kills the channel faster than a dull one. A number the
   creator only guessed ("like four") stays out. Patterns that need a missing fact (an age, a
   client's name) are skipped, not invented.
5. Never copy an example. Copy its shape.

## Rules that hold across the whole set

- **Odd, exact numbers beat round ones.** `$124,794` not "over $100K". `1256 leads` not "1,000+".
  `16 minutes` not "fast". Exact reads as true.
- **The parenthetical is the most-used device.** It carries the differentiator or the proof:
  `(Without Sales Calls)` · `(Full Course)` · `(with AI)` · `(If I Had to Start Over)` ·
  `(The Exact Math)` · `(Nobody Does This Right)` · `(Copy My Exact System)`.
- **A timeframe caps the cost of watching.** `in 16 minutes` · `in 26 Minutes` · `in 12 minutes`
  · `In 92 Mins`. It also promises density.
- **Lowercase is a real style,** not a mistake: it reads like a personal note, not a media product
  (Matt Gray's 49x and 4.4x, Ali Abdaal's 4.4x, Tom Youngs). Offer one lowercase option.
- **Talk to one person.** "If you're ambitious but inconsistent" beats "for entrepreneurs".
- **Under 60 characters,** payoff in the first 5 words: the end gets cut off on phones.
- **The thumbnail is a second hook, not a repeat** (see Thumbnails).

## The patterns

### 1. "If you want X, copy this" / "If you're X, watch this" (call-out)
Names the viewer's exact goal or identity, then hands over the answer. The single strongest
pattern in the set for fitness and self-improvement coaches.
- 9.4x · If You Want To Lose 20 Lbs By Christmas, Copy This Diet (coachdango)
- 7.3x · If you want to drop up to 20 pounds in a month, copy this (coachdango)
- 6.0x · If you're ambitious but inconsistent, please watch this (aliabdaal)
- 4.1x · If you want to eliminate your visceral fat in 30 days, copy this (coachdango)
- 2.0x · If you're ambitious but feel lost, please watch this (realmattgray)
- 1.3x · If You Want To Make Millions With Webinars In 2026, Watch This (jasonfladlien)
Shape: `If you want [specific result] [by when], copy this` · `If you're [trait] but [struggle], please watch this`

### 2. The exact result
A real, unrounded number in the title. Works when the creator has the number.
- 193.7x · How To Make $100k/Month With 96% Profit (jkmolina)
- 5.8x · How I Made $124,794 Last Month Selling $27 Digital Products (danhenry)
- 5.6x · The $10K/Month Plan (takimoore)
- 3.1x · How I generated 1256 qualified leads in 48 hours, for $0.27 (Taki Moore)
- 2.8x · This Sell-By-Chat Script Got Me To $1,000,000/Year (jkmolina)
- 1.3x · How I gained 914,000 LinkedIn followers (and made $1.2M/month) (Matt Gray)
Shape: `How I [result with exact number] [doing specific cheap/simple thing]`

### 3. Outcome + parenthetical
The result, plus a bracket that removes the thing everyone assumes is required, or proves density.
- 9.4x · How I Make $1.6M A Month (Without Sales Calls) (Taki Moore)
- 6.2x · How To Build A $20k+/m Coaching Or Consulting Offer (Full Course) (Tom Youngs)
- 3.1x · How to Learn So Fast People Assume You're Naturally Gifted (with AI) (aliabdaal)
- 2.7x · How to Design Webinar Slides (Nobody Does This Right) (jasonfladlien)
- 2.1x · the EXACT path to $100k/m (without funnels or sales calls) (tomyoungschannel)
- 1.8x · How To Retire Before You Hit 30 (The Exact Math) (charliemofficial)
Shape: `How to [outcome] ([without the painful thing] | Full Course | with [tool])`

### 4. Age or identity first
"I'm 46." does the targeting: the right viewer self-selects in one word.
- 5.1x · I'm 46. These are the only 8 exercises you need to build a lean physique. (coachdango)
- 2.9x · I'm 47. These 12 Health Purchases Changed My Life. (coachdango)
- 2.3x · I Made $40M By 40. My Advice To Young Entrepreneurs (danhenry)
- 1.7x · I'm 46. Here's how I healed my chronic low back pain. (coachdango)
Shape: `I'm [age]. [The only N things / how I fixed X].`

### 5. The experiment or the conversation (story opener)
Something happened, and the viewer wants to know how it ended.
- 21.8x · I Asked a YouTube Millionaire Why My Videos Don't Grow (danhenry)
- 18.8x · I Wore An Apple Watch, Oura, Whoop, And Fitbit Air But Did Not Expect This.. (coachdango)
- 16.1x · A Billionaire Gave Me Advice That Made Me Millionaire (danhenry)
- 4.2x · I Tried Fully Automating My Video Editing With AI (RAW RESULTS) (aliabdaal)
Shape: `I [tried / asked / tested] [specific thing] [and an unexpected turn]`

### 6. The withheld answer ("this")
Promises a big payoff and points at it without naming it. Needs a strong, specific video behind it.
- 15.2x · If You Only Watch One Personal Brand Video, Make It This (realmattgray)
- 4.5x · you'll win at business as soon as you do this (tomyoungschannel)
- 4.5x · This Weird Marketing Strategy Made Me $45,000,000 (danhenry)
- 3.9x · An Extreme Way To Get Extremely Rich! (charliemofficial)
- 1.9x · THIS is replacing the course industry?! (jkmolina)
Shape: `[You'll get result] as soon as you do this` · `This [odd adjective] [thing] got me [result]`

### 7. Timeframe compression
A lot of value in a stated, short time.
- 49.4x · how to BEAT the new LinkedIn algorithm in 16 minutes (Matt Gray)
- 2.8x · Brutally Honest Advice About Productivity in 26 Minutes (aliabdaal)
- 1.9x · everything you need to do to make your home feel 10x healthier in 12 minutes (coachdango)
- 1.4x · Watch Me Live Scale This $1.5M/Mo Business In 92 Mins (jeremyhaynestraining)
- 1.3x · It took me 37 years to realize what I'll tell you in 18 minutes. (alexhormozi)
Shape: `[Everything / how to] [outcome] in [exact minutes]`

### 8. The hypothetical reset
"If I were starting over" gives the whole roadmap without promising the viewer a result.
Good for creators with no big numbers yet.
- 2.4x · How I Would Build a $10M Service Business (If I Had to Start Over) (alexhormozi)
- 1.9x · If I Wanted to Build a Personal Brand, I'd Do This (realmattgray)
- 1.4x · How I Would Build a Personal Brand in 2027 (If I Had to Start Over) (alexhormozi)
- 1.2x · How I'd Build an 8 Figure Business (If I Had to Start Over) (realmattgray)
Shape: `If I wanted [result], I'd do this` · `How I'd [build X] (If I Had to Start Over)`

### 9. "X is hard until you Y"
Agrees with the viewer's struggle first, then sells the fix.
- 2.4x · Personal Branding Is Hard Until You Build Systems Like This (realmattgray)
- 2.4x · your goals keep failing until you run this meeting (realmattgray)
- 1.2x · Life Is Hard Until You Design It Like This (realmattgray)
Shape: `[Thing they find hard] is hard until you [do this]`

### 10. Contrarian or "the new era"
Kill a belief or name a shift. Works when the creator really thinks it. Note: "X is dead" is
worn out in business niches; use the shape with fresher words.

- 2.4x · The New Era of Meta Ads Has Officially Begun (jeremyhaynestraining)

Shape: `Stop [common advice], do this instead` · `[Popular thing] sucks, actually`

## Thumbnails

- **Two hooks, two jobs.** The title carries the outcome. The thumbnail carries the fight, the
  scale or the open loop. Examples: title "How I Make $1.6M A Month (Without Sales Calls)" →
  thumbnail "SALES CALLS ARE FOR LOSERS?". Title "How To Build A $20k+/m Coaching Offer
  (Full Course)" → thumbnail "6 hours...".
- **A real face, mid-gesture or mid-reaction,** never a stiff pose. Every outlier sampled had one.
- **2-4 words,** big enough to read at phone size. One word in a colour.
- **Red for the enemy, a strikethrough on the old way** (~~SALES CALLS~~) is a strong device.
- **Show the size of the prize:** a big number, a before → after (`0 → 846K`), or how much is
  inside ("6 hours...").
- **Contrast with the feed:** a bright colour or a clean dark background stands out among busy
  thumbnails. Three subjects at most (face, text, one object).

## Titles that underperform in this niche

- Vague "ultimate guide / everything you need to know" with no number, enemy or person.
- Hype words: "unlock", "game-changer", "secrets revealed", "you won't believe".
- Three devices crammed in: number + parenthetical + timeframe + emoji.
- A promise the first 30 seconds don't start to pay off.
